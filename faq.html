FAQ

Quick answers.

If your goal is “fun, not spam,” this is the page most admins read.

Will this spam my channel?

Spawns can be public, but outcomes can be private. That’s the core design.

Can spawns expire / despawn?

Yes. Despawn timers keep the channel from getting stuck with old spawn messages.

Can I choose where spawns happen?

Yes. Configure one or more channels depending on how your server is organized.

Is there an admin setup flow?

That’s what config commands are for. Keep the gameplay out of regular channels.
