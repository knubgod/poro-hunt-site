Support

Help keep Poro Hunt online.

Poro Hunt is free to use. If you enjoy it, donations help cover hosting and ongoing development. Donations are optional and do not unlock gameplay features.

Donate on Ko-fi Support Server

What donations cover

Hosting costs, monitoring, uptime, and time spent improving the bot. If the bot grows, costs grow with it — donations keep it sustainable.

No pay-to-win

Donations do not provide gameplay advantages, exclusive catches, boosted odds, or locked features. Everyone plays the same game.

Optional thank-you (safe perks)

If you want, you can offer non-game perks like a “Supporter” role in a support server, or a name listed on a credits page — but no in-game power.

Transparency

Keep your donation messaging honest: “help cover hosting” is better than overpromising. It protects you and builds trust with users.
