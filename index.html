Discord Mini-Game — Collection • Spawns • Rewards

Catch poros. Build a collection. Keep your server lively — without the spam.

Poro Hunt is a lightweight Discord mini-game. A poro spawns in your channel, people try to catch it, and results can be private to keep the chat clean.
